# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-25

### Added
- Initial release with 35 tools across 8 categories
- **Example AgTech backend** — Axum + SQLite with seeded Kenyan farm data (coffee, wheat, tea, maize, avocado)
- Fields: list, get, create, update, delete (5 Kenyan fields with GPS, soil type, elevation)
- Crops: list, get, plant, calendar, log_harvest (SL28 coffee, Kenya Eagle wheat, TRFK tea, H614D maize, Hass avocado)
- Activities: log, list, summary (fertilizer, spraying, weeding, plucking, irrigation)
- Weather: 7-day forecast, historical, alerts, growing degree days
- Satellite: NDVI, crop health assessment, anomaly detection, field boundaries
- Market: commodity prices (Nairobi Coffee Exchange, Mombasa Tea Auction, NCPB), history, trends, best sell time
- IoT: sensors, soil moisture (multi-depth), rainfall gauges
- Yield: estimation, harvest history, season comparison
- Alerts: pest alerts (Coffee Berry Borer, Fall Armyworm), disease risk (CBD, Leaf Rust)
- All intelligence data realistic for Central/Western Kenya growing conditions
